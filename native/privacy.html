Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2023. This policy outlines our commitment to protecting the personal data of our global partners, customers, and website visitors.

1. Data Collection 2. How We Use Data 3. Data Protection 4. Cookie Policy 5. International Transfers 6. Your Rights 7. Contact Information

PT ARISYAFOOD GLOBAL NIAGA ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it through our compliance with this policy. This policy describes the types of information we may collect from you or that you may provide when you visit our website and our practices for collecting, using, maintaining, protecting, and disclosing that information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect several types of information from and about users of our website, including information by which you may be personally identified, such as:

  • Identity Data: Name, company name, and job title.
  • Contact Data: Email address, telephone number, and physical business address.
  • Transaction Data: Details about products you have inquired about or purchased from us.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type and version, time zone setting, and location.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use information that we collect about you or that you provide to us to ensure our business operations remain efficient and professional:

  • To provide you with information, products, or services that you request from us.
  • To fulfill our obligations and enforce our rights arising from any contracts entered into between you and us.
  • To notify you about changes to our website or any products or services we offer or provide.
  • To maintain the security and integrity of our supply chain and logistics operations.

security 3. Data Protection Measures

We have implemented measures designed to secure your personal information from accidental loss and from unauthorized access, use, alteration, and disclosure. All information you provide to us is stored on our secure servers behind firewalls. We utilize industry-standard encryption for data at rest and in transit.

4. Cookie Policy

Our website uses cookies to distinguish you from other users. This helps us to provide you with a good experience when you browse our website and also allows us to improve our site. You can set your browser to refuse all or some browser cookies, or to alert you when websites set or access cookies.

5. International Data Transfers

As a global exporter based in Indonesia, information we collect may be transferred to, stored, and processed in various countries where we have business partners or service providers. By using our services, you consent to this transfer of information outside of your country of residence.

6. Your Legal Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • The right to access and receive a copy of your data.
  • The right to rectification of any inaccurate personal data.
  • The right to erasure ('right to be forgotten') in certain circumstances.
  • The right to object to processing for marketing purposes.
